## Research claim

**Human Agency** is treated here as a law-state problem rather than a slogan. The chapter asks which observations are admissible, which transformations are lawful, what authority is required for consequence, which receipts establish standing, and what replay or transfer would be required to keep that standing over time. The Chateco position is deliberately bounded: adjacency is not equivalence, execution is not proof, and absence of evidence does not become refusal by narration.

## Preserved source development

Human action also begins with partial observation. Attention is bounded, memory is selective, interpretation is socially conditioned, and concurrent thought capacity is finite. The thesis denotes the actionable cognitive bound by \\(\\kappa\\). This does not reduce persons to processors; it marks the practical fact that reality must be projected into a form a person can admit and act upon.

Alcoholics Anonymous supplies a mature staged process. The Twelve Steps do not begin with unbounded control. They begin with admission of a condition, movement toward a source of restoration, decision, inventory, disclosure, readiness, repair, continued inspection, prayer and meditation, and carrying the result to others. Each stage manufactures conditions required by later stages.

Recovery can therefore be examined as observation repair. Inventory makes excluded or distorted relations visible. Sponsorship provides an external admission and falsification surface. Meetings provide testimony and replay. Amends convert internal intention into enacted consequence. Daily practice reruns the equation rather than treating recovery as one completed artifact.

The mapping is structural, not reductive. Rust typestates are not a model of personhood, grace, moral responsibility, or spiritual agency. They provide a precise contrast: just as a plan is not authorization, intention is not an amend; just as a log is not a receipt, self-description is not the whole social consequence.
