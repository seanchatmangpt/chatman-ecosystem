# 61. The Post-AGI Execution Equation

The operational frame of the book can be written as one bounded pipeline:

\[
parse \rightarrow route \rightarrow admit/refuse \rightarrow diagnose/repair \rightarrow construct \rightarrow actuate \rightarrow receipt \rightarrow replay \rightarrow standing
\]

Each arrow is a typed transition.

## Parse

Convert raw input into candidate structured meaning without granting it authority.

## Route

Identify the relevant capability, ontology, subject, and owning system. Routing is selection, not execution.

## Admit or refuse

Align the observation to the exact subject, ground it, bound it, evaluate constraints, and preserve typed refusal when the transition is not lawful.

## Diagnose and repair

When an admitted path fails, locate the failing transition, propose the narrowest repair, and preserve the failure as new evidence. Do not rerun unchanged failures without a new hypothesis.

## Construct

Manufacture reversible artifacts, plans, proofs, synthetic worlds, interfaces, or intents. Construction may be massive and parallel.

## Actuate

Only BRCE crosses into consequential DO. The authority broker evaluates the exact subject and operation.

## Receipt

Bind identity, authority, execution, result, postcondition, and causal evidence.

## Replay

Preserve enough context to reconstruct manufacture and re-evaluate consequential decisions without blindly repeating them.

## Standing

Calculate the strongest bounded claim supported by the evidence. Do not promote narrative confidence into ALIVE.

## Recursive operation

The result becomes new observation:

\[
O_{t+1}=Observe(Reality_{t+1})
\]

The loop therefore evolves both the world and the civilization memory describing how to interact with it.

## Falsifier

The calculus is bypassed if any external mutation can enter after `construct` and before `receipt` without passing operational admission and BRCE.

## Operational exercise

Trace one existing end-to-end automation through all nine transitions. If two steps are collapsed, identify whether the collapse is a lawful optimization or a hidden authority/evidence boundary.
