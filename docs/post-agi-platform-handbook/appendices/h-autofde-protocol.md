# Appendix H — AutoFDE Reality Acquisition Protocol

## 1. Parse

Identify the bounded domain, requested outcome, and explicitly excluded operations.

## 2. Observe

Acquire repositories, schemas, cloud state, process evidence, documentation, events, human statements, and other lawful observations. Preserve source identity and freshness.

## 3. Align

Resolve identity relationships without collapsing ambiguous objects.

## 4. Map ontology

Reuse public ontology first; add custom terms only for genuine local semantics.

## 5. Admit

Produce `O*` with explicit unknowns, conflicts, and refusal conditions.

## 6. Construct

Manufacture candidate integrations, models, or remediation plans. Do not actuate.

## 7. Experiment

Project the environment into GymAct where feasible and gather dynamic evidence.

## 8. Operational admission

Submit the selected exact candidate and authority requirements to BRCE.

## 9. Observe again

Capture postconditions and OCEL events.

## 10. Close the class

If the pattern is reusable, extract ontology, ggen pack, validators, gym, receipts, and applicability conditions into civilization memory.
