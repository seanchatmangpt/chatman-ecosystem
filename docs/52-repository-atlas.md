# Repository Atlas

This atlas describes the **major constitutional and release roles** of the Chatman Ecosystem. It is not a claim that these are the only repositories in the wider portfolio. The composition root intentionally distinguishes the release graph from the larger discovery universe.

## Core role atlas

| Role | Primary repository | What it owns | What it must not silently own |
|---|---|---|---|
| constitutional composition | `chatman-ecosystem` | exact component identity, dependencies, release standing, completion frontier | component implementation or ambient DO |
| public ontology | `open-ontologies` | shared semantic supply and reusable vocabulary | runtime standing of consumers |
| semantic manufacture | `ggen` | deterministic graph-to-artifact manufacture | planning choice or consequential authority |
| pack distribution | `ggen-marketplace` | reusable qualified manufacturing law | consumer runtime truth |
| formal certification | `mfact` | formal/mechanical certification surfaces | permission to actuate |
| config admission | `star-toml` | exact configuration grammar/admission | downstream process authority |
| exploration/planning | `autofde-lab` | planners, formalism experiments, benchmark evidence, reversible search | consequential DO |
| product planning/control | `autofde` | product-level planning/control composition | inherited standing from dependencies |
| bounded allocation | `bcinr` | CMCA-style allocation contracts | orchestration or actuation authority |
| orchestration | `mfw` | process/tool orchestration | world mutation outside BRCE |
| actuation | `gymact` | provider/world admission, BRCE consequence, verify, receipt | planning authority by transport or capability |
| process type law | `wasm4pm-compat` | type/format compatibility and structural connector law | runtime I/O authority |
| process execution | `wasm4pm` | executable process/mining capabilities | canonical ontology ownership |
| process research | `process-intelligence` | evidence-governed process intelligence | self-certification of exact runtime standing |
| provenance | `affidavit` | evidence/standing provenance surfaces | policy or world actuation |
| fleet conformance | `praxis` | cross-project composition and conformance experiments | automatic admission of discovered projects |
| capstone | `fdegym` | Fortune-5 FDE world/capstone composition | release standing by aspiration |

The v26.9.1 release manifest binds each required role to an exact repository/ref/SHA. Those bindings are release-instance facts, not timeless architecture. A future release may select a different exact subject or even a different implementation while retaining the constitutional role.

## The ggen family

### `ggen`

The manufacturer. It consumes admitted semantic state and emits deterministic consequences. Its value compounds when downstream repositories stop hand-authoring representations that can be derived from the graph.

### `ggen-marketplace`

The reusable law registry. This is where a successful local pattern becomes distributable capability: pack identity, ontology, templates, gates, qualification fixtures, provenance, and evidence policy.

### `ggen-legacy`

The recovery/compiler frontier. Legacy formats, source trees, documents, APIs, and historical artifacts are observations to be normalized and admitted, not evidence that old architecture must be retained. The key distinction is between preserving information and preserving obsolete implementation structure.

### `ggen-create`

Pack authoring belongs conceptually upstream of generation. Its role is to help create/discover pack law, not to become a second generator architecture.

## The planning and process family

### `AutoFDE-Lab`

The research plane: planners, benchmarks, state-space experiments, formal interchange, planner leagues, and benchmark evidence. Its outputs are candidates and comparative evidence.

### `AutoFDE`

The product/control plane: takes admitted planning machinery toward an operational forward-deployed engineering product. It composes dependencies but may not inherit their standing by name.

### `POWL`

Partial-order workflow semantics. POWL is useful because the world is not naturally one serial checklist. Independent activities should remain concurrent; precedence should exist only where causality requires it.

### `MFW`

Orchestration. MFW bridges selected/planned work into executable ordering while maintaining exact step identity and boundaries to upstream allocators and downstream actuators.

### `BCINR`

Allocation/certification. CMCA-style outputs are useful precisely when represented as a narrow, bounded contract instead of a hidden optimizer with universal authority.

## The world and evidence family

### `GymAct`

The actuation kernel and gym interface. A world can be synthetic or live, but consequential transitions remain authority-gated and receipt-bound.

### `wasm4pm-compat`

The structural compatibility plane. Its strongest guarantee is what it does **not** do: structural descriptors and macros do not open files, sockets, databases, processes, or networks merely because they describe those transports.

### `wasm4pm`

The process execution/mining plane. It is where process models, event evidence, conformance, statistics, and autonomic mechanisms can be executed against exact subjects.

### `process-intelligence`

The evidence-driven analysis plane. Process intelligence treats execution evidence as a first-class basis for recommendation and closure rather than allowing generative systems to invent a plausible history.

### `Affidavit`

The provenance/standing evidence plane. It can say what evidence exists and how it binds to a subject; it does not thereby acquire policy or DO authority.

## The formal and graph family

### `mfact`

Formal certification. This is part of the system’s resistance to “the generated artifact looks plausible, therefore it is admitted.” Formal proofs and mechanical courts narrow the set of lawful promotions.

### QLever

Graph-scale execution substrate. The constitutional graph must remain queryable at scales where an in-memory toy example would no longer be persuasive. QLever is a replaceable substrate whose standing comes from exact executed queries and independent oracles.

## Gym portfolio

The gym family provides bounded worlds and benchmark surfaces. Representative lines include FDE, SRE, cloud, governance, church/life, protocol, ChatGPT/ClaudeCode, games, and synthetic strategic planning. Their purpose is not to claim that simulation equals production. Their purpose is to create exact, replayable, adversarial environments in which planners and policies can be tested before any live authority is considered.

The canonical topology is:

```text
DISCOVER/PRESERVE -> SELECT/PLAN -> ADMIT -> DO -> VERIFY -> RECEIPT -> REPLAY
```

Discovery can be global and permissive. DO cannot.

## Repository archaeology

The wider portfolio contains prototypes whose original implementation context may be obsolete while some semantic insight remains useful. The default recovery strategy is therefore not “merge every dormant branch” and not “rewrite every old project.” It is:

1. observe the exact old artifact;
2. identify the still-valid semantic delta;
3. separate that delta from obsolete ancestry and local assumptions;
4. lift reusable law into ontology/pack/process/verifier form when possible;
5. reconstitute against the current architecture;
6. verify the new exact subject independently;
7. preserve the historical artifact as provenance, not authority.

This turns a large repository portfolio from a maintenance liability into a searchable option graph.

## A repository is not a standing claim

The most important atlas rule is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Repository existence} \not\Rightarrow \text{Capability standing}}
\]

A repository can be valuable as source archaeology, a benchmark, an ontology supply, a rejected experiment, or a future candidate without being ALIVE. The composition root should preserve those distinctions rather than flatten every repository into “active” or “dead.”
