# 31. Intelligence at the Frontier

**Executive thesis:** Post-AGI architecture should spend intelligence on novelty and turn solved novelty into a deterministic substrate as quickly as evidence allows.

## The frontier moves

What is novel today may be routine tomorrow. A new cloud API, regulation, attack pattern, product model, or scientific finding initially requires interpretation. Once the organization stabilizes a bounded understanding, continued model inference becomes cognitive rent unless the environment itself remains genuinely uncertain.

## Promotion and demotion

Knowledge should be promoted from candidate to admitted law only with evidence. It should also be demoted when evidence becomes stale, provider behavior changes, contradictions appear, or a falsifier breaks. Determinism is not dogma; it is conditional on the admitted boundary remaining valid.

## Why AGI does not remove governance

Greater intelligence can search more of the possibility graph and discover better constructions. It does not automatically grant authority or make external consequences self-verifying. Candidate → admitted → executable → consequence observed → independently verified → replayed remains a useful ladder even when the candidate generator is extraordinary.

## Operating practice

Maintain a frontier backlog separate from routine execution. Spend agent budget on unresolved semantic classes. When a frontier item closes, encode the result and remove it from the recurring cognition queue. When a contract drifts, reopen the class explicitly rather than silently falling back to inference.

## Diagnostic question

What has recently moved from novel to known but still consumes model inference on every instance?
