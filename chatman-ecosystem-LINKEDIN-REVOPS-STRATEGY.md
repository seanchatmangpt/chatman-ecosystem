# chatman-ecosystem LinkedIn RevOps Strategy

## Standing and claim ceiling

`chatman-ecosystem` is the composition root and standing/authority control plane. It should not duplicate LinkedIn publication, CRM implementation, process mining, or pack semantics owned by child repositories. Its LinkedIn RevOps role is to bind **identity, relationships, evidence, standing, authority, receipts, and release-level composition** across the revenue system.

Current strategy standing: **COMPOSITION / PARTIAL_ALIVE**. No ecosystem-wide `ALIVE` claim follows from any individual strategy document or child capability.

## Challenger Sale doctrine

The cross-repo commercial invariant is:

```text
Teach -> Tailor -> TakeControl -> Diagnose -> Quantify -> Prove
```

**Teach:** code generation is becoming cheap while synchronization, verification, governance and evidence remain expensive.

**Tailor:** project that thesis into the buyer's actual role, system and constraints.

**Take Control:** move from generic interest to one bounded Software Manufacturing Readiness assessment and then one falsifiable Enterprise Manufacturing Proof.

The ecosystem must refuse the weaker narrative that 10,000 commits alone proves enterprise value.

## SELECT / CONSTRUCT / DO

The composition root must preserve authority separation across channels:

- content selection and account prioritization are SELECT;
- posts, documents, CRM payloads, messages, meeting intents and POV plans are CONSTRUCT;
- LinkedIn publication, outbound communication, CRM mutation, calendar mutation, contract execution and customer-system actuation are DO.

`can_do(x) != may_do(x)` applies to marketing and sales exactly as it applies to infrastructure.

All consequential integrations should carry exact subject identity, intent digest, scope, authority identity, consequence observation and receipt.

The lineage must support multi-touch reality without inventing causation. First-touch, last-touch and influence are separate projections; none should be mislabeled as causal proof without an experiment that supports that claim.

## Ecosystem Definition of Done for LinkedIn RevOps

A bounded LinkedIn RevOps release may be called `ALIVE` only when the declared required components each independently hold the required standing against exact admitted subjects, and the mandatory composition edges are observed.

At minimum, a production claim would require:

1. an evidence-bound content asset;
2. an observed publication event;
3. an executed assessment/handoff path;
4. deterministic Person/Account reconciliation;
5. an executed CRM projection if that projection is in scope;
6. process-event export and conformance execution;
7. replayable evidence linking the exact campaign objects;
8. explicit authority evidence for any consequential external actions.

A strategy Markdown file satisfies none of those runtime conditions.

## Falsifiers

The ecosystem LinkedIn RevOps claim is invalidated if publication cannot be observed, account identity drifts across systems, qualification is inferred without evidence, CRM/process data cannot be reconciled, revenue provenance is fabricated, a child component's standing is borrowed by another subject, or any live external action bypasses its authority boundary.

The business goal is not maximum LinkedIn automation. It is a standing-bearing path from teaching signal to verified customer value with the minimum necessary manual synchronization.
