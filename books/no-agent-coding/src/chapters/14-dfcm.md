# 14. DfCM: Maximize Options, Then Minimize Execution

**Executive thesis:** Design for Combinatorial Maximalism preserves the largest lawful reversible option graph before admission, then drives the admitted execution path toward combinatorial minimalism.

## Why maximalism comes first

Early optimization often destroys options before enough evidence exists. DfCM keeps reversible possibilities represented: candidate tools, routes, providers, projections, ontologies, plans, and constraints. One failed edge is topology information, not proof that the graph is exhausted.

## Why minimalism follows admission

A rich possibility graph should not become a rich active queue. Once a path is admitted, execution should be narrow, dependency-closed, deterministic, and easy to receipt. This resolves the apparent tension between broad exploration and Little’s Law: graph cardinality is not the same as operational WIP.

## Post-AGI interpretation

More intelligence increases the number of possibilities that can be explored. Without DfCM, that can increase chaos. With DfCM, intelligence expands the reversible frontier while admission and authority keep realized consequences small and explicit. AGI does not skip checkpoints; it can search them faster.

## Operating practice

Represent everything relevant. Admit almost nothing. Finish what is admitted. Preserve excluded alternatives as graph knowledge rather than spawning active work for every possibility.

## Diagnostic question

Are you confusing a large represented possibility graph with a large active work queue?
