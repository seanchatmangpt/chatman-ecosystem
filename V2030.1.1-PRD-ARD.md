# Chatman Ecosystem v2030.1.1 — PRD / ARD

**Repository:** `seanchatmangpt/chatman-ecosystem`  
**Role:** constitutional composition control plane  
**Target release:** v2030.1.1  
**Document class:** target product/architecture requirements  
**Standing conferred by this document:** `UNKNOWN` — specification and PR existence are not implementation evidence.

## 1. Purpose

Chatman Ecosystem v2030.1.1 SHALL be a general software-manufacturing control system in which admitted observations, ontology, reversible possibility search, deterministic manufacture, formal/runtime admission, bounded authority, actuation, receipts, replay, and standing remain distinct but composable.

The constitutional equations are:

```text
A = μ(O*)
R = receipt(A)
```

and the operational law is:

> **Zero unreceipted actuation.**

This repository governs composition identity, dependency closure, authority, evidence aggregation, and release standing. It is not a source-code monorepo and SHALL NOT absorb the implementation authority of its component repositories.

## 2. North-star product requirement

The v2030.1.1 system SHALL make the following request technically executable without granting ambient authority to a model, planner, proof, hook, workflow, connector, or generated artifact:

> **Here is the outcome, exact subject, authority boundary, budget, risk tolerance, and policy. Determine the maximal lawful reversible possibility space, select an admitted plan, manufacture the required artifacts, execute only the authorized consequences, verify the postconditions, and return replayable receipts.**

The user-facing abstraction SHALL move from files/tickets/provider-specific integrations toward intent, constraints, economics, ontology, authority, and standing.

## 3. Constitutional invariants

v2030.1.1 SHALL preserve these invariants across every component and projection:

1. `O != O*`: observation does not become admitted truth by transport or model confidence.
2. `SELECT != CONSTRUCT != DO`: planning, manufacturing, and consequential execution are distinct authority classes.
3. Capability and credentials do not imply authority.
4. Hooks and LLM outputs manufacture intents/candidates only.
5. Generated artifacts are projections, not canonical semantic authority.
6. Simulation/emulation/inspection do not imply live execution.
7. Formal proof establishes only the formal claim it binds; it does not grant runtime authority.
8. `ALIVE` is calculated from required exact-subject evidence, never assigned narratively.
9. Provider/cloud/marketplace schemas are projections of canonical capability/commercial semantics, not owners of those semantics.
10. Every consequential mutation crosses the broker/BRCE boundary and produces a verifiable receipt.
11. Replay observes equivalence and SHALL NOT silently re-actuate.
12. One failed or unsupported edge is topology; it does not invalidate independent lawful paths.

## 4. DfCM product law

The ecosystem SHALL implement Design for Combinatorial Maximalism (DfCM): preserve the maximal reversible lawful possibility graph before irreversible selection.

```text
observed possibilities
  -> ontology/capability bounds
  -> policy bounds
  -> authority bounds
  -> cost bounds
  -> evidence/falsifier bounds
  -> admitted reversible frontier
  -> selection
  -> manufacture
  -> authorized consequence
```

Premature provider/framework/marketplace selection is a design defect when equivalent capability-level alternatives remain lawful.

## 6. Cloud and marketplace architecture

Clouds and marketplaces SHALL be modeled as capability/contract projections rather than product forks.

```text
CanonicalCapabilityGraph -> ProviderProjection(provider)
CanonicalCommerceGraph   -> MarketplaceProjection(marketplace)
```

The architecture SHALL be capable of representing materially different IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, data, AI, enterprise-application, private, regional, and sovereign ecosystems without privileging AWS, Microsoft, or Google as canonical roots.

Marketplace targets include, where their contracts are admitted and supported, AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, Oracle, IBM, SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Databricks, Alibaba, Atlassian, GitHub, private enterprise catalogs, and future providers.

Marketplace readiness SHALL separately model discovery, publishing, identity, procurement, contract, entitlement, fulfillment, deployment, metering, billing, private offers, lifecycle, reconciliation, receipt, and replay. A provider limitation is `UNSUPPORTED`; a policy/authority denial is a typed refusal. Neither may be converted into success.

## 10. Repository-level acceptance law

Each component's PRD/ARD defines its owning acceptance/falsifier boundary. The composition root SHALL NOT replace those courts with a central green badge.

For a component to reach `ALIVE`, its exact admitted subject must satisfy the evidence required by its own constitutional/architectural boundary, including applicable positive behavior, negative/refusal behavior, integration, exact-head verification, persisted evidence, receipt integrity, replay, exclusions, and drift checks.

A component PR being open, mergeable, reviewed, or green is not sufficient.

## 11. Composition-level acceptance law

A v2030.1.1 crown may be calculated only when:

1. every required component is resolved to an exact repository/ref/SHA/tree identity;
2. dependency edges close over those exact identities;
3. ontology/generation/formal/runtime/authority/receipt ownership boundaries remain intact;
4. each component has evidence from its owning acceptance court for the exact admitted subject;
5. required CLI/API/MCP/A2A projections preserve one capability semantics and authority class;
6. cloud/marketplace claims bind real admitted provider contract units and, where live behavior is claimed, real observed execution;
7. no simulated or predecessor-head evidence is promoted to current exact-subject standing;
8. all consequential DO has explicit authority, broker routing, observed postcondition, and receipt;
9. receipt DAGs verify and replay according to their declared replay class;
10. generated projections correspond to their pinned canonical source;
11. every required rail names the same composed release subject;
12. the exact release head passes the root crown/admission court.

Only then may the composition root derive crown standing. This document cannot assign it.

## 12. Required Fortune 5 capstone

At least one crown scenario SHALL demonstrate a bounded, real end-to-end enterprise task spanning materially different provider families while preserving canonical semantics. The scenario SHALL include:

- environment discovery and exact subject identity;
- admitted ontology/world model;
- multiple candidates where alternatives lawfully exist;
- hard economic/security/compliance/region/authority constraints;
- deterministic/replayable manufacture;
- formal/runtime admission appropriate to each claim;
- authorized BRCE actuation;
- live postcondition verification;
- marketplace or procurement projection where applicable;
- process/evidence telemetry;
- receipt DAG closure;
- failure/refusal/unsupported evidence;
- replay or explicit bounded non-replayability.

A demo that only generates code, passes CI, validates manifests, or succeeds in simulation is insufficient.

## 13. Reconstitution and continuous manufacturing

Every required component SHOULD converge on a machine-readable reconstitution contract sufficient to recover its purpose, ontology, dependencies, build/manufacture path, interfaces, tests, admission, authority, deployment, evidence, release, and recovery behavior.

Fleet conformance SHALL detect stale/missing ontology, generated drift, protocol divergence, unreceipted actuation, unreconstructable projects, stale evidence, and governed WIP. Repair remains CONSTRUCT until the owning repository admits and authorizes it.


Machine speed does not weaken authority. Faster autonomous repair increases the requirement for exact subject identity, policy bounds, deterministic/replayable evidence, and fail-closed DO.

