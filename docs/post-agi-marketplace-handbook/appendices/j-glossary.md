# Appendix J — Marketplace Engineering Glossary

**Agreement** — Accepted commercial obligation between parties, usually derived from an offer/order.

**ALIVE** — Evidence-backed standing for an exact subject after required observed execution and verification.

**BRCE** — Bounded Receipted Controlled Execution; the exclusive path for consequential DO.

**Canonical commercial graph** — Marketplace-independent identity and semantics for product, plans, rights, usage, lifecycle, and evidence.

**Commercial portability** — Preservation of canonical commercial meaning across different marketplace projections.

**CONSTRUCT** — Authority class that manufactures reversible artifacts or immutable intents without external consequence.

**DO** — Authority class that changes consequential external state.

**Entitlement** — Durable representation of rights granted by an admitted agreement.

**Fulfillment** — Operational realization of an entitlement.

**Marketplace pack** — Executable knowledge bundle for projecting canonical commercial semantics into a marketplace.

**Meter batch** — Admitted, content-addressed aggregation of measured usage for submission.

**O** — Candidate observation.

**O\*** — Bounded and admitted observation.

**Offer** — Proposed commercial terms; not yet an accepted agreement.

**Projection** — Marketplace-specific representation of canonical semantics.

**Receipt** — Evidence binding exact subject, authority, intent, consequence, verification, and replay.

**Reconciliation** — Proof-oriented matching of usage, submissions, charges, fees, settlement, and internal records.

**SELECT** — Authority class for choosing among admitted possibilities.

**Settlement** — Marketplace/payment-system movement or accounting of funds.

**Standing** — Typed evidence claim over an exact subject and capability.

**UNSUPPORTED** — Capability absent from the bounded implementation or market surface; distinct from refusal.

**Typed refusal** — Explicit rule-based rejection such as `REFUSED:AUTHORITY_MISSING`.

**Vendor extension** — Marketplace-specific semantic that remains outside the canonical core unless equivalence is proven.
