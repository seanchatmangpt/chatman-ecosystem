# 33. Executive Metrics for the Deterministic Enterprise

**Executive thesis:** Once code output is abundant, leaders need measures that reveal closure, drift, authority, and reuse rather than activity alone.

## Rediscovery rate

How often does a human or model reconstruct a decision the organization has already made? High rediscovery means knowledge remains trapped in people, prompts, tickets, or implementation details rather than compiled into reusable law.

## Admission lead time and WIP

Measure time from candidate to admitted terminal state and the number of admitted-but-unfinished items. These expose the real closure bottleneck. Candidate volume should not be mistaken for throughput.

## Projection and replay coverage

Track what proportion of recurring surfaces are deterministically derived from admitted semantics and what proportion of consequential claims can be replayed from exact identity. These measures show whether the factory is replacing manual synchronization and narrative trust.

## Agent-retirement rate

Count semantic classes that move from recurring interpretation to deterministic handling while preserving an exception frontier. The goal is not maximum automation; it is minimum unnecessary cognition subject to safety, authority, and adaptability.

## Diagnostic question

Can your executive dashboard distinguish candidate volume from admitted closure?
