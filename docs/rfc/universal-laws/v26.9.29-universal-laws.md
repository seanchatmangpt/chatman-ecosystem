# Universal Laws — Formal Core

Status: proposed executable standard. Scope: framework-neutral architecture qualification.

## Objective

A system compounds qualified machine experience when admitted observations and reproduced failures become reusable constraints, reducing fresh intelligence required for comparable future work without expanding hidden semantics or authority.

The standard is intentionally independent of language, framework, runtime, vendor, or model.

## Descriptive theory

### Qualified value and NULL

Let a candidate capability be (C), an exact subject be (S), and NULL be the qualified system with (C) absent.

[
V_q(C mid S)=G(C mid S)-D_{sem}-D_{auth}-C_{requal}-C_{op}
]

where:

- (G) is qualified outcome gain;
- (D_{sem}) is semantic/interpretive debt;
- (D_{auth}) is authority or consequence-surface debt;
- (C_{requal}) is future qualification cost introduced by the capability;
- (C_{op}) is operational cost required to preserve the claim.

Normalize (V_q(NULL mid S)=0). A capability is **negative intelligence** when:

[
V_q(C mid S) < V_q(NULL mid S)
]

A locally impressive capability can therefore be worse than nothing when its apparent gain is dominated by hidden semantics, duplicated ownership, widened authority, requalification burden, or operational fragility.

### Compounding

For a comparable task family, let (I_n) be fresh intelligence required at episode (n), and (Q_n) the reusable qualified constraints available before that episode.

A system compounds experience when increasing qualified constraints reduces fresh intelligence without silently widening the claim:

[
rac{partial I}{partial Q} < 0
]

subject to exact-subject identity or explicit bounded qualified equivalence.

### MX0 and MXinf

**MX0** is the failure mode where comparable work repeatedly consumes approximately the same fresh intelligence because evidence and failure history are not distilled into reusable qualified structure.

**MXinf** is the failure mode where each new capability increases future interpretation, coupling, or requalification burden faster than it contributes qualified value. MX0 and MXinf are architecture diagnostics, not computational-complexity classes.

### Semantic closure

For a claim surface (K), semantic closure requires every load-bearing predicate (p) to be one of:

[
p in ADMITTED cup EXCLUDED cup UNKNOWN
]

and UNKNOWN MUST NOT be silently coerced to true or success. Closure is bounded to the claim; it is not a claim of omniscience.

### Canonical ownership

For every load-bearing predicate (p) in scope (sigma):

[
|Owner(p,sigma)| = 1
]

Other representations are deterministic projections, references, caches, or evidence; they do not become canonical by repetition.

### Architectural hardening

Architectural hardening is **constraint distillation**: reproduced invalid states are converted into the smallest reusable rule, type, schema, refusal, court, or runtime boundary that removes that invalid path while preserving lawful adjacent behavior.

BEAM/OTP is a non-normative existence proof that mature failure, lifecycle, supervision, and process semantics can embody accumulated negative knowledge. No runtime is required by this RFC.

## Normative laws

**L1 — No Hidden Semantics.** Every fact that can change admission, selection, construction, authority, consequence, verification, replay, or standing MUST be represented in canonical state or typed UNKNOWN.

**L2 — Canonical Ownership.** Each load-bearing semantic predicate MUST have one canonical owner per scope. Other forms MUST be projections, references, or explicitly non-authoritative caches.

**L3 — Consequence Separation.** SELECT, CONSTRUCT, and DO are distinct transitions. Crossing a phase boundary MUST NOT manufacture authority.

**L4 — Non-Authority of Artifacts.** Evidence, Signature, Plan, Capability, Qualification, Receipt, Replay, and Standing assessment MUST NOT imply Authority.

**L5 — Exact Subject.** Standing belongs to the exact admitted subject. Transfer to a non-identical subject requires explicit bounded qualified equivalence and its own falsifier.

**L6 — Semantic Lineage.** Derived load-bearing state MUST identify its canonical source and derivation. Generated projections MUST NOT become canonical through manual editing.

**L7 — Bounded Consequence.** Consequential transitions MUST bind subject, independently admitted authority, intended bounds, intended consequence, observed outcome, receipt, and replay identity. UNKNOWN outcome MUST NOT become success without reconciliation.

**L8 — Edge-Local Failure.** Failure of one edge removes or repairs that edge. It MUST NOT imply global STOP while another required lawful edge remains reachable.

**L9 — Reverse Chesterton.** Replacement of a mature mechanism MUST preserve its recovered constraints or provide a same-subject falsifier proving those constraints unnecessary.

**L10 — Negative Chesterton.** A guard, prohibition, awkward interface, or apparently redundant constraint SHOULD be treated as possible accumulated negative knowledge until its function is recovered or falsified.

**L11 — Adversarial Constraint Distillation.** A reproduced failure SHOULD become the smallest durable reusable constraint that prevents the failure class while preserving lawful adjacent behavior.

**L12 — Qualification Does Not Promote Authority.** Conformance, proof, test success, simulation, replay, receipt, or standing assessment MUST NOT issue DO authority.

## Consequence algebra

The allowed conceptual order is:

[
SELECT ightarrow CONSTRUCT ightarrow DO ightarrow OBSERVE ightarrow VERIFY ightarrow RECEIPT ightarrow REPLAY
]

Authority is an independent input to DO, not an output of SELECT, CONSTRUCT, evidence, qualification, or receipt.

## Conformance levels

Levels are cumulative.

- **UQ0 EXPLICIT** — load-bearing semantics represented or typed UNKNOWN.
- **UQ1 OWNED** — UQ0 plus canonical ownership and semantic lineage.
- **UQ2 SEPARATED** — UQ1 plus SELECT/CONSTRUCT/DO and non-authority separation.
- **UQ3 EXACT** — UQ2 plus exact-subject identity and bounded equivalence.
- **UQ4 CONSEQUENCE** — UQ3 plus bounded consequence, UNKNOWN reconciliation, receipt, and replay.
- **UQ5 ADVERSARIAL** — UQ4 plus executable falsifiers and anti-vacuity observation.
- **UQ6 COMPOUNDING** — UQ5 plus failures distilled into reusable constraints and observed reduction of repeated fresh intelligence for a comparable task family.
- **UQ7 HARDENED** — UQ6 plus inherited qualification for new capabilities without duplicated semantic ownership.

No UQ level grants DO authority.

## Anti-vacuity

A falsifier contributes qualification evidence only when the attempted adversarial condition is observed:

[
AttemptObserved = true
]

A positive witness is meaningful when the attack was attempted and the prohibited violation was not observed:

[
AttemptObserved land 
eg ViolationObserved
]

A test that never exercised the load-bearing transition is not a passing falsifier.

## Required falsifier families

A conformance suite MUST exercise at least:

1. hidden semantic decision input;
2. conflicting canonical owners;
3. SELECT performing DO;
4. evidence/receipt/qualification self-granting authority;
5. standing transferred to another subject without bounded equivalence;
6. derived state with missing lineage;
7. UNKNOWN coerced to success;
8. edge failure promoted to global STOP despite reachable lawful work;
9. mature mechanism replaced without recovered constraints or same-subject falsifier;
10. guard removed without recovered purpose or falsifier;
11. reproduced failure not converted into durable constraint;
12. qualification issuing authority;
13. capability whose qualified value is below NULL;
14. bounded qualified equivalence as a positive control.

## Standing and exact subject

Conformance establishes only predicates observed against the exact subject under the named verifier/evidence horizon. It does not automatically transfer standing to another revision, environment, verifier, runtime, or consequence.

Canonical evidence chain:

[
observed ightarrow admitted ightarrow executed ightarrow changed ightarrow verified ightarrow receipted ightarrow replayable
]

## Adoption rule

Prefer **reuse > composition > extension > invention**. Invention requires a falsifier showing existing qualified machinery cannot satisfy the required predicate.

A new capability is not an improvement merely because it performs a local task better. It is an improvement only when its qualified value is not below NULL and it preserves the applicable laws above.
