# 18. Packs as Accumulated Executable Knowledge

**Executive thesis:** A ggen pack should represent a solved class of work, not a pile of snippets.

## From instance closure to class closure

Solving one deployment, schema, API, or compliance integration is useful. Encoding the reusable semantics, gates, templates, falsifiers, receipts, and replay rules turns that instance into a class-level capability. Future work becomes instantiation rather than rediscovery.

## Marketplace as memory

ggen-marketplace can therefore be understood as accumulated executable knowledge. Distribution is not only source distribution. It is distribution of bounded semantic contracts and manufacturing recipes that can be independently qualified before use.

## Why qualification matters

A pack that emits plausible files but lacks source identity, admission gates, negative fixtures, real consumer verification, or deterministic replay is scaffolding. The pack should advertise the strongest level it has earned and refuse stronger claims.

## Operating practice

When a recurring engineering pattern is solved, do not stop at a runbook. Ask whether the solution can become a pack with ontology, queries, constraints, templates, fixtures, proof or verification surfaces, receipts, and replay. The economic goal is zero rediscovery for known classes.

## Diagnostic question

Which solved instance is ready to become a reusable class-level pack?
