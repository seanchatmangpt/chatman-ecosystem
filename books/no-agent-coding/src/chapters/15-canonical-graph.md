# 15. The Canonical Graph

**Executive thesis:** The enterprise cannot become deterministic while every repository and department privately owns the meaning of shared concepts.

## Graph as semantic IR

A canonical RDF graph can represent identities, capabilities, dependencies, constraints, provenance, authority, processes, economic attributes, and evidence links independently of any one programming language or runtime. It functions as an intermediate representation for the enterprise rather than a documentation diagram.

## Public semantics first

Shared public vocabularies reduce private reinvention. PROV-O, DCAT, DCTERMS, SKOS, SHACL, ODRL, QUDT, SOSA and domain standards can anchor common meaning where appropriate. Custom ontology should express the genuine remainder, not rename a public concept for local convenience.

## Why repositories change role

Repositories remain important implementation and evidence boundaries, but they stop owning enterprise concepts merely because a class or schema happens to live there. A repository becomes one bounded projection or witness of a capability contract.

## Operating practice

For a cross-cutting concept such as entitlement, deployment, incident, customer, capability, or authority, locate every local definition. Choose or extend public semantics, establish a canonical identity, and project local representations from that source instead of synchronizing them manually.

## Operational significance

**15. The Canonical Graph** is not retained as a label-only reference. This page defines a simulation contract rather than a claim that simulated success equals reality. A gym world must state its entities, state variables, actions, observation projections, information partitions, roles, policies, objective functions, authority boundaries, stochastic processes, and termination conditions. Without those dimensions a score is uninterpretable because the benchmark does not say what information or power the policy had.

## System contract

The useful algebra is `Episode = World × Roles × Policies × InformationPartitions × Authority`. Planner, policy, role, and agent remain distinct: a planner proposes; a policy maps admitted observations to candidate actions; a role describes responsibilities; an agent is an actor with bounded capabilities. Reward is evidence about the objective encoded by the environment, not permission to actuate outside it.

## Failure modes and falsifiers

Simulation is falsified by reality-model mismatch, leakage of privileged observations, an action projection that grants authority the real system does not have, reward hacking, nondeterministic fixtures without recorded seeds, or a scenario suite that excludes the failure class being claimed. The output should therefore include world identity, seed, policy identity, observation/action projections, result metrics, and a receipt that lets another runner reproduce the episode.

## Evidence before promotion

For this subject, promotion requires evidence that intersects the claim: exact subject identity, the admitted inputs or assumptions, the verifier or observation boundary, and a reproducible result. Static structure can establish representational closure; simulated execution can establish bounded behavior; neither is silently promoted to real-world consequential standing. A changed subject, stale observation, failed replay, unresolved contradiction, or verifier that no longer intersects the claim revokes the prior standing and requires re-admission.
