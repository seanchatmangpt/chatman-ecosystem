# Projection Policy

The Markdown manuscript in `src/` is the canonical authored source for this book. It is not a generated projection and may be edited directly.

`book.contract.toml` is the executable structural contract for the manuscript. `scripts/verify.py` checks navigation closure, chapter and appendix counts, duplicate and orphan targets, placeholder hygiene, and required doctrine coverage. The mdBook HTML output under `book/` is generated and must not be committed as source authority.

The book describes Chatman Ecosystem doctrine but does not promote any component standing beyond current exact-subject evidence. Examples and equations are architectural exposition unless a chapter explicitly binds a live receipt.
