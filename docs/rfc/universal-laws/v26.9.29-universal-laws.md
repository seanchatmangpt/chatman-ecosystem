# Universal Laws — Normative Core

Status: candidate. Scope: framework-neutral architecture qualification.

## Objective
A system compounds qualified machine experience when admitted observations become reusable constraints, reducing fresh intelligence required for comparable future work.

Compounding target: d(required_intelligence)/dt < 0.

NULL is absence of the candidate capability while preserving the qualified system. A candidate is negative when its qualified outcome gain is outweighed by semantic debt, risk, authority expansion, or requalification cost relative to NULL.

## Normative laws
1. No Hidden Semantics: every fact that can change admission, selection, construction, authority, consequence, verification, replay, or standing MUST be represented in canonical state or typed UNKNOWN.
2. Canonical Ownership: each load-bearing semantic predicate MUST have one canonical owner per scope. Other forms are projections, references, or non-authoritative caches.
3. Consequence Separation: SELECT, CONSTRUCT, and DO are distinct. Transition between them MUST NOT manufacture authority.
4. Non-Authority: Evidence, Signature, Plan, Capability, and Receipt MUST NOT imply Authority.
5. Exact Subject: standing belongs to the exact admitted subject. Transfer requires explicit bounded qualified equivalence.
6. Semantic Lineage: derived load-bearing state MUST identify canonical source and derivation. Generated projections MUST NOT become canonical through manual editing.
7. Bounded Consequence: consequential transitions MUST bind subject, authority, bounds, intended consequence, observed outcome, receipt, and replay identity. UNKNOWN outcome MUST NOT become success without reconciliation.
8. Edge-Local Failure: failure of one edge removes or repairs that edge; it MUST NOT imply global STOP while a required lawful edge remains reachable.
9. Reverse Chesterton: replacement of a mature mechanism MUST preserve its recovered constraints or provide a same-subject falsifier proving them unnecessary.
10. Negative Chesterton: a guard, prohibition, or awkward constraint SHOULD be treated as possible accumulated negative knowledge until its function is recovered or falsified.
11. Adversarial Constraint Distillation: reproduced failures SHOULD become the smallest durable rule/type/schema/test/refusal that prevents the failure class while preserving lawful adjacent behavior.
12. Qualification Does Not Promote Authority: conformance, proof, test success, simulation, replay, or standing assessment MUST NOT issue DO authority.

## MX diagnostics
MX0: repeated comparable tasks require approximately constant fresh intelligence because observations are not distilled into reusable qualified structure.

MXinf: each capability increases future interpretation or requalification burden through private semantics, duplicate ownership, hidden coupling, or expanding authority.

These are architecture diagnostics, not computational-complexity classes.

## Semantic closure
A claim surface is semantically closed when every load-bearing predicate is admitted and lineage-bound, explicitly excluded, or typed UNKNOWN under a policy that cannot silently treat UNKNOWN as true. Closure is claim-relative and bounded.

## Conformance
UQ0 EXPLICIT: load-bearing semantics represented or UNKNOWN.
UQ1 OWNED: UQ0 plus canonical ownership and lineage.
UQ2 SEPARATED: UQ1 plus consequence and non-authority separation.
UQ3 EXACT: UQ2 plus exact-subject identity and bounded equivalence.
UQ4 CONSEQUENCE: UQ3 plus bounded consequence, reconciliation, receipt, replay.
UQ5 ADVERSARIAL: UQ4 plus executable falsifiers and anti-vacuity observation.
UQ6 COMPOUNDING: UQ5 plus failures distilled into reusable constraints and measured reduction in repeated intelligence.
UQ7 HARDENED: UQ6 plus inherited qualification for new capabilities without duplicated semantic ownership.

Levels are cumulative.

## Hardening
Architectural hardening is accumulation of qualified constraints that reduce reachable invalid states while preserving lawful useful states. It is not synonymous with generic security.

BEAM/OTP is a non-normative existence proof that mature lifecycle, supervision, and failure semantics can encode accumulated negative knowledge. No runtime is mandated.

## Required falsifiers
A conformance suite MUST test hidden semantic decision input; conflicting canonical owners; SELECT performing DO; evidence or receipt self-granting authority; evidence transferred to another subject; UNKNOWN coerced to success; edited generated projection treated as canonical; edge failure treated as graph failure despite a reachable lawful alternative; mature mechanism replaced without constraint analysis; and a locally successful candidate whose value is below NULL.

## Standing
Conformance establishes only predicates observed against the exact subject. It does not transfer standing to another revision, environment, verifier, or consequence.

Canonical evidence chain:
observed -> admitted -> executed -> changed -> verified -> receipted -> replayable

## Adoption
Prefer reuse over composition over extension over invention. Invention requires a falsifier showing existing qualified machinery cannot satisfy the required predicate.
