# 30. Retire Agents, Not People

**Executive thesis:** The strategic target is the retirement of repeated interpretation from known work, not the removal of humans from the enterprise.

## Agent retirement function

A recurring class moves through discover → formalize → admit → manufacture → verify → replay. Once that path is stable, normal instances can bypass the agent. Exceptions and novel cases return to the frontier. This is a continuous process, not a one-time automation project.

## Why people gain leverage

Experts shift from being service stations for repetitive decisions to authors of operational law, falsifiers, ontologies, and exception policies. Their knowledge becomes capital that can be reused across time and repositories. The organization depends less on individual memory while making expert judgment more durable.

## Why AI still matters

AI is exceptionally useful for broad observation, hypothesis generation, ontology proposal, contradiction detection, search, and exploration. Those are frontier activities. The design error is to make a probabilistic agent the permanent runtime for a rule that has already become known and bounded.

## Operating practice

Pick one queue where senior engineers repeatedly answer the same question. Capture the decision class, encode its evidence and refusal rules, manufacture the normal answer deterministically, and route only exceptions back to experts.

## Diagnostic question

Which recurring expert decision should become executable law rather than a faster queue?
